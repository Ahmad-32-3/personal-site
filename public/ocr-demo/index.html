Watch the Pipeline Read a Page

Pick a sample page or upload your own, and run it. The pipeline runs on this computer and shows each stage: the lines it finds, the glyph boxes in a line, and what it recognized in each box.

Page

Works best on printed Khojki pages like the 1896 print. Handwritten pages won't work.

1

Finding the Lines

The page is turned into black ink on white, and each band of ink inside the printed frame becomes one line.

2

Finding the Glyphs

Each connected piece of ink is boxed, and pieces stacked on top of each other are grouped into one glyph.

3

Recognizing Each Glyph

  • Green: recognized, 95% sure or more. Written as text.
  • Orange: a best guess, written into the text but not certain.
  • Red: no good guess. The best guess is under 30%.
